## Full description

QALQON is a 2D tower defense strategy game where your placements reshape the enemy route. Turn open ground into a maze, hold narrow approaches and build overlapping fields of fire.

Defend eight original maps against ground troops, aircraft and railway bosses. Begin with rapid Gatling fire, slowing Goo, explosive Missiles and chaining Tesla attacks. Add map-specific Flame, Mortar, Laser and Ice towers. Each tower has three levels and configurable targeting priorities.

Survive 100 waves in Classic and Extended, or chase your personal best in Endless. Choose from three difficulties, unlock new battlefields and continue your saved battle.

The core game works offline. English and Uzbek interfaces are included. Original geometric visuals and short sound effects are generated through code.
