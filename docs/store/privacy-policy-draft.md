# QALQON privacy policy — draft

This draft must be reviewed by the publisher against the final distributed build. Publisher identity, support contact and effective date are not yet supplied.

QALQON's core gameplay is offline. Local game settings, campaign progress, records and one resumable battle are stored on the device. The game does not require a game account and its intended scope includes no advertising, purchases inside the game, chat, public leaderboard or analytics server.

The Steam edition may use Steam services for achievements, platform language and cloud synchronization of profile/progress. Steam handles that platform data under its own privacy terms. An active battle is not synchronized between Android and Windows.

Local progress can be removed by deleting the application's stored data. Cloud copies, where enabled, are managed through Steam. The publisher must specify the support channel for privacy questions and document any diagnostic collection added before release.

Do not claim a completed platform data-safety declaration from this draft alone. Audit the final dependencies, permissions, network behavior and platform integrations before answering the store forms.
