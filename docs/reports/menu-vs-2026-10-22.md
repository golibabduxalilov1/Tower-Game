# Integrated VS emblem

The supplied cutout was edited with built-in imagegen to add exactly `VS` between the rifle and opposing turret muzzle. Gold beveled letters with navy edges touch both weapon tips. The soldier, round three-gun tower and shared navy ground remain recognizable. The separate bottom `ASKAR VS MINORA` caption was removed to avoid duplication.

Versioned source and exact prompt: `assets/art-source/menu-native-duel-v2-vs.png` and adjacent JSON. The existing runtime key `menu:duel-native-hd` now loads this version; the importer prefers v2 and retains v1 as a source fallback.

Actual game renders at 1280×720, 1920×1080 and 1200×540 are in `artifacts/menu-vs/`. Desktop and touch renders show legible centered lettering, contact at both tips and no menu-control overlap. Control bounds and behavior are unchanged.
