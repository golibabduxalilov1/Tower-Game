# Supplied nature scenery

Replaced the small tiled procedural forest and identical edge-tree rows with native supplied foliage. Forest edges mix full canopies, berry bushes and shrubs; desert edges mix palms and cactus shapes. The top/bottom uses the supplied wooden-fence garden strip. Placements vary deterministically per map, source upscaling is capped, and side decorations avoid entry/exit approaches and buildable grid cells. Dark grass bands now blend gradually into the field. No obstacles were added to the16×12campaign.

Validation:51asset dimensions/alpha layers and456native tower frames checked. Actual MonoGame captures inspected in `artifacts/nature-design/battle.png`, `sand.png`, and `phone.png` at1280×720and1920×1080touch preview. Source foliage crop rectangles were corrected to masked silhouettes after rendered inspection. Windows and Android Release builds checked; physical Android hardware not tested.
